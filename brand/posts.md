## 1 — What it is

The share. And the drip.

Every Robinhood stock token pays its dividends by growing your balance. HALVES splits that growth off into its own token.

SPY → pSPY (the share, at maturity) + ySPY (every dividend until then). Merge back any time.
[link]

## 2 — What you gain

Two tokens, two choices:

→ Keep pSPY: one SPY share delivered on 31 Dec 2027.
→ Keep ySPY: every dividend SPY reinvests until then, claimable as it accrues.
→ Hold both: merge into SPY whenever you want. No fee, no lockup.

Your tokens never leave your wallet except to split.
[link]

## 3 — How it works

1. Split: deposit a stock token, receive one principal token and one yield token per share.
2. Claim: yield-token holders claim the dividend growth, pro rata, any time.
3. Redeem: at maturity the principal token redeems the share; the yield token redeems what is left.

Series today: SPY, NVDA, AAPL, TSLA. No owner, no admin key.
